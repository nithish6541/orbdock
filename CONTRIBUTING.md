# Contributing to Orb

Thanks for your interest! Orb is maintained by [@nithish6541](https://github.com/nithish6541), who reviews and merges every change.

## How to contribute

1. **Fork** this repository and create a branch for your change.
2. Make your change and check it builds and runs: `./build.sh --run`.
3. **Open a pull request** against `main` describing what you changed and why. Screenshots or a short screen recording help a lot for anything visual.
4. The maintainer will review it, and may ask for changes before merging.

Nobody pushes to `main` directly; every change arrives through a pull request.

## What fits Orb

Orb is deliberately tiny: no windows, no menus, no controls. Just the Dock taking on the colors of what's playing. Changes that make that core experience calmer, more beautiful, more reliable or lighter on resources are very welcome. Changes that add settings, UI or features beyond it probably won't be merged, so if you're unsure, open an issue to discuss first.

## Reporting bugs

Open an issue with your macOS version, what you expected, and what happened. For visual problems, a screenshot of the Dock helps.
